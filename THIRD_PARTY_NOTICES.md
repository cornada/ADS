# Third-Party Notices

## Policy
- Permissive licenses (MIT/BSD/Apache): OK to vendor/submodule or depend on.
- GPL/AGPL: do NOT mix into core unless you want your repo to become GPL.
- Missing LICENSE file: treat as reference-only (read & re-implement ideas).

Record for each reused repo:
- URL
- commit hash
- license
- what modules were reused (paths)
